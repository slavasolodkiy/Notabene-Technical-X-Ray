## Metadata-only sources

Public Notabene documentation, OpenAPI captures, presentation definitions, package metadata, legal pages, and standards material are not redistributed as raw corpus files unless a supportable redistribution license was identified. Their corpus entries are metadata: title, original URL, retrieval information, checksum of the dated research capture where available, and a withheld reason. A public URL is not itself permission to redistribute.

Government and standards links are retained as primary-source pointers, without making a blanket copyright claim across jurisdictions. NPM tarballs and other repositories captured during research are not automatically part of the portable redistribution merely because they were downloadable.

The manifest's `available` flag means raw content is included. `metadata_available` means only the metadata artifact is included. These flags are deliberately not interchangeable.
